# Surface brief: mapa en vivo (pantalla principal de la PWA)

Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.

The person on the street needs, in the first seconds, both what is happening around them and the way to report (equal weight, confirmed by the user). Must read in direct sunlight; must not feel alarmist or red everywhere.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The city's incidents read like the Mi Teleférico network: every category is a line with its own colour, every report is a station on it. Refuses the category default of red pins on a grey map with a blue floating plus.

OWN-WORLD: White station-panel ground and near-black ink; the five category line colours (amarilla, roja, morada, café, naranja) used only as line and station inks, never as page fills; a thin multicolour line strip under the header as the network legend; one humanist, highly legible sans (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next) at a fixed scale; ring stations (open ring = activo, filled = confirmado, struck = vencido).

STORY: The visitor sees which lines run near them and how far, understands state at a glance, and reports in two taps without typing.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with name and live status, network strip below it; the map fills the middle with ring-station markers clustered by area; bottom panel «Cerca de ti» drawn as a vertical line diagram (each row a station on its category line with distance and expiry); full-width «Reportar» button pinned at the bottom of the panel, equal in weight to the list.

FORM: Señalética de Mi Teleférico, the user-pinned direction (top of the first round's grounded list); seed: seed:15988994b3e15d73.
